# B.o.B.W. v0.2 — Purpose

## Department identity

**B.o.B.W. — Best of BOTH Worlds** is the ingestion department at the front of the Content-Factory.

It is a local-first **Customs Agent and warehouse sorter** for accumulated source material: links, transcripts, ChatGPT archives, documents, scans, images, notes, datasets, and other “ore.” Its job is to make incoming material legible to both people and machines while retaining the trail back to the supplied source.

## Purpose statement

> B.o.B.W. receives source material, inspects its origin and handling status, issues a traveler-provenance record, and proposes a safe Content-Factory destination without allowing derived or uncertain material to become canonical by accident.

## What B.o.B.W. owns

- source intake and preservation
- source identity and SHA-256 verification
- capture context and human intent
- OCR, transcription, translation, and other transformation lineage
- human-readable and machine-readable paired records
- initial material classification
- proposed Content-Factory routing
- visible processing events and retry/failure states

## What B.o.B.W. does not own

- final truth judgment
- silent deletion or replacement of source material
- automatic canonical promotion
- final editorial authority
- rights clearance without review
- production release or Shipping Dock approval

Those responsibilities remain downstream or human-gated.

## Attribution
