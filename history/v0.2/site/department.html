What it does

Backlog→Customs inspection→Traveler record→Warehouse sort→Factory assay

Customs Agent

Records what arrived, where it came from, when and why it was captured, its checksum, processing lineage, rights and privacy status, and whether the source remains preserved.

Warehouse Sorter

Proposes raw_artifact, reusable_material, active_project, archive, waste, or quarantine. Every route remains proposed until human review.

Boundaries

  • No silent deletion or overwrite
  • No automatic canonical promotion
  • No rights clearance by assumption
  • No product publication before downstream review and Shipping Dock authority
