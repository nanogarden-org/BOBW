What

B.o.B.W. is a dual-ingestion agent architecture. It accepts narrative material—notes, documents, transcripts, scans—and structured material—YAML, JSON, CSV, metadata—and keeps both representations linked to the same evidence.

Why

Human-readable prose carries explanation, uncertainty, rationale, exceptions, and lived meaning. Machine-readable records carry stable identifiers, constraints, validation, routing, and repeatable processing. Durable AI systems require both.

The promise

A reader can see where a record came from. A model can use it without guessing its identity, confidence, or status.
