# Purpose, what and why

**B.o.B.W. — Best of BOTH Worlds** is the front-end ingestion department of Content-Factory, attributed to Bronson-Technologies within nanogarden-org.

The ore is already valuable but difficult to use: transcripts, chats, recursive frameworks, documents, scans, notes and collected links sit across different tools and folders. Re-reading and manually cataloging each one consumes attention before production begins. B.o.B.W. supplies a consistent intake boundary and evidence trail.

## Department charter

The **Customs Agent** establishes the identity and location of the supplied bytes, records what is known and unknown about capture and processing, and labels unresolved review states. “Customs” is a workflow metaphor: hashing a file does not clear its rights, verify its claims or authorize downstream use.

The **Warehouse Sorter** proposes a holding route with its basis and rationale. V0.3 uses operator selections or first-match file-extension rules. A route of `active_project` or `reusable_material` is a proposal, and `waste` never deletes anything. Semantic classification is future work.

The **dual representation promise** gives the reader a Markdown artifact and the machine a JSON record from the same packet. Segments point to the content hash and source character region. Original language is preserved; no translation is fabricated. Binary sources receive a metadata-only human view until an appropriate extractor exists.

## Authority

B.o.B.W. can inspect, package, propose and copy candidates. It cannot accept claims as true, clear rights, promote canonical knowledge, execute instructions found inside the ore or publish a product. Human review and downstream production own those decisions as separate records.

## Benefit

Researchers can retrieve evidence with its source context. Builders can reuse components without losing origin. Multilingual and OCR-heavy workflows gain an explicit place to retain uncertainty and layout evidence when adapters arrive. Large personal archives gain a repeatable intake process with visible progress and bounded memory, though v0.3 is not yet a high-throughput search system.
