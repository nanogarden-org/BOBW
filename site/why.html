Why preserve both worlds?

Home What Why Where How

A clean paragraph helps a reader. A stable identity, locator and transformation history help a machine. Keeping these together makes accumulated source material easier to review and reuse.

Visible actions reduce guessing

Each successful packet and candidate copy has a console event and a durable record. Failures name their source. Reruns create new observations, leaving prior context available.

Evidence survives convenience

Moving a file does not change its content identity. Original-language text stays intact. Processing time cannot masquerade as capture time, and a routing proposal cannot masquerade as acceptance.

Build toward multilingual and OCR intake

Future extractors need source regions, language and uncertainty. V0.3 does not yet perform OCR or translation; that priority remains explicit.
