Customs Agent

Identify supplied bytes, retain their source location, and record what is known about capture and transformation. Unknown capture dates and unassessed rights stay visible.

Warehouse Sorter

Propose raw artifact, reusable material, active project, archive, waste or quarantine. V0.3 uses an operator selection or an extension rule. No route automatically approves or deletes material.

Two views, one packet

The human reads Markdown. The machine reads JSON. Both carry the same source identity and evidence segments. A separate receipt records a verified local copy.
